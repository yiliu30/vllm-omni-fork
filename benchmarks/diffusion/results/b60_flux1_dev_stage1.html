Key Findings

  1. bf16 requires TP=4 on B60 — TP=1 OOMs at load, TP=2 OOMs during inference (1024×1024).
  2. W4A16 fits single GPU at 19.8 GB peak (of 24.4 GB available).
  3. bf16 TP=4 beats W4A16 TP=4 — 18.26s vs 19.01s (4% faster). Unlike 5090 where W4A16 was faster, BF16 wins on B60 likely due to XPU's bf16 hardware support advantage over INT4 dequant overhead.
  4. TP scaling on B60: TP=1→2 gives 1.31× speedup, TP=2→4 gives 1.20×. Diminishing returns but still useful.
  5. B60 is 2.3–3.7× slower than 5090 depending on config. Gap narrows with more TP (less compute-bound).
  6. No continuous batching — throughput is constant at ~0.034 qps regardless of concurrency.
  7. XPU driver caution — --stage-init-timeout 900 required; default 300s times out during bf16 loading.

    --omni --tensor-parallel-size 4 --port 8099 \
    --disable-log-stats --stage-init-timeout 900

# 2. Wait for health
until curl -s http://localhost:8099/health; do sleep 5; done

# 3. Run benchmark
$PYTHON benchmarks/diffusion/diffusion_benchmark_serving.py \
    --backend vllm-omni --base-url http://localhost:8099 \
    --model /home/yiliu7/models/FLUX.1-dev-AutoRound-w4a16 \
    --task t2i --dataset vbench --num-prompts 10 \
    --width 1024 --height 1024 --num-inference-steps 20 \
    --max-concurrency 1 --warmup-requests 1 --warmup-concurrency 1 \
    --output-file results/w4a16_tp4_c1.json
