5. Key Findings

  1. bf16 requires TP=4 on B60 — TP=1 OOMs at model load, TP=2 OOMs during 1024×1024 generation. Only TP=4 works (22.0/24.4 GB, 90% utilization).
  2. W4A16 enables single-GPU inference at 19.8 GB peak — viable on B60 with 4.6 GB headroom.
  3. bf16 TP=4 is 4% faster than W4A16 TP=4 (18.26s vs 19.01s) — opposite of 5090 where W4A16 won. XPU's native bf16 compute is stronger than INT4 dequantization overhead.
  4. At 512×512, bf16 and W4A16 converge (6.26s vs 6.19s) — compute is so fast that overhead dominates.
  5. B60 is 2.3–3.7× slower than 5090. Gap narrows with more parallelism (TP=4: 2.26×) and widens at TP=1 (3.65×).
  6. TP scaling: W4A16 TP=1→2 = 1.31×, TP=2→4 = 1.20×. Diminishing returns due to communication overhead on PCIe.
  7. No continuous batching — throughput is constant regardless of concurrency (0.033–0.056 qps depending on TP).
  8. Latency scales linearly with steps and ~3× with 4× resolution increase (sub-quadratic due to non-attention overhead).
  9. XPU driver caution: requires --stage-init-timeout 900; concurrent GPU access can deadlock the driver (fix: modprobe -r i915 && modprobe i915).
